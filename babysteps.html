BabySteps

An AI parenting app for first-time parents. It brings a daily log, growth tracking, age-aware guides and a memory vault into one place, and it adapts regional guidance to the country the parent lives in.

My role
Designed the app in Figma and developed it using the Claude API and speech-to-text.
Context
Personal project, presented at Northeastern Expo 2026.
Status
Working web app.
Tools
Figma, Claude API, speech-to-text

Problem and user

New parents collect advice from many places, and much of it ignores where they live. Food guidance and financial advice change by country, yet most baby apps give one answer to everyone. BabySteps is for first-time parents who want tracking, guidance and keepsakes in one app.

Key decisions

  • Design in Figma before building. The flows and screens were settled first, so structure could change while changes were cheap.
  • Let the parent's country set the defaults. Choosing India switches the app to metric units, the IAP vaccine schedule and WHO growth standards. Other countries get their own units and regional guidance. Advice that ignores country was the problem I started from.
  • Keep tracking and memory-keeping in one app. A daily log, growth tracking and age-aware guides sit next to a memory vault with locked time-capsule letters, so parents do not need a second app for keepsakes.
