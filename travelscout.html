TravelScout

A group travel-planning chatbot that is still in development. I conducted the user research. Development covers personalized itineraries, friend connections and polls.

My role

Problem and user

Planning a trip with friends usually means options scattered across chats. TravelScout is meant to keep the group's preferences, options and decisions in one place, with a chatbot that helps the group plan.
