Team enablement · Slides

Project management fundamentals training

Tools only stick when the team shares a vocabulary. I wrote an 18-slide primer on what a project manager does and how a project moves from idea to closing.

Role
Wrote and designed the deck
Format
18 slides
Audience
Colleagues new to formal project management
Companion
A cross-team briefing on the V-model
Skills shown
Project management fundamentals, Agile and predictive methods, business analysis, training development, stakeholder communication

What it covers

The deck starts with what makes something a project instead of operations, then what a project manager actually does. It compares predictive, adaptive and hybrid life cycles and walks through the five stages from ideation to closing, naming the artifact each stage produces.

Methods

One slide set covers plan-based work with baselines and change control. Another covers Scrum and Kanban, with the artifacts each one uses. A slide on tailoring asks four questions that help a team choose, and notes that most real programs blend the two.

Business analysis

The last section shows how requirements get elicited, analyzed, documented and validated, which ties back to the traceability workbook.

Why I wrote it

The deck was built to onboard the wider team, so it keeps jargon low and ends each section with the artifact a person would actually produce. It sits beside the other tools here, since a template works better when people share the words behind it.
