Lean Six Sigma program dashboard

A Lean Six Sigma program needs to know who holds which belt, who is working toward the next one, and where every improvement project sits in DMAIC. I built the workbook that answers all three on one page.

Role
Started it on my own initiative
Built in
Excel
Tracks
Belts, projects and savings
Skills shown
Lean Six Sigma, DMAIC, process improvement, KPI dashboards, program reporting

The problem

Belt records and project status lived in separate lists. Nobody could say how many people were mid-way to a Green or Black Belt, because those belts require an exam and a completed project before they are awarded.

What I built

Three tabs. The belt tab follows a training-matrix layout, with a completed flag and a completion date for each of six levels: White, Yellow, Green, Black, Master Black and Champion. The project tab records name, manager, area, status, cost savings and completion date, and its status follows the DMAIC stages. The dashboard rolls both up.

Decisions along the way

Green and Black Belts show two states, working toward and certified, so the dashboard can chart the pipeline as well as the results. A single in-progress tile became one tile per DMAIC stage, because "in progress" told leaders nothing about where projects were stuck. Dates use one format, directors come from a dropdown, and charts sit on the left with tables on the right on every tile row.

What it shows

New belts per month, total belts by level, belts in progress, projects by stage and savings by area. Everything updates from the two data tabs.
