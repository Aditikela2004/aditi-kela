The app has no usage baseline in this document. Targets below are proposals to test, not forecasts. To confirm

Position and audience

  • Position: the baby app that fits the country you live in. Proposed
  • First audience: first-time parents in the US and India, plus parents living away from their home country. Proposed
  • Secondary audience: grandparents and partners who contribute to a shared time capsule. Proposed

North Star metric

Weekly active families: families who log at least three days in a week. It rewards a habit, not a signup. Proposed

Growth loops

  • Shared capsule loop: a parent invites a grandparent, the grandparent adds a letter, the parent returns to read and invites others. Proposed
  • Country loop: a parent living abroad shares the country schedule with another parent from the same country. Proposed

Tracking needed

  • Events: signup, profile created, first log, log per day, guide opened, vaccine schedule opened, capsule created, invite sent, invite accepted.
  • A weekly dashboard of the five metrics above.
  • Privacy: events carry no health details, only counts.

Risks

  • Parents are tired and time-poor, so onboarding must be very short.
  • Health content is sensitive. One wrong schedule can undo trust.
  • Free competitors cover everyday logging. Growth depends on the country angle and family sharing.
