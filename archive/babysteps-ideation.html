Problem statement

First-time parents need one place to track their baby's days, understand health and growth, and keep memories, but the advice they find ignores the country they live in. How might BabySteps give each parent guidance that fits their country and keeps the early months easy to record? Confirmed

How might we

  • HMW show the right vaccine schedule and growth standard for the country a family lives in?
  • HMW make logging a feed or diaper take two taps?
  • HMW give financial and savings guidance without sounding like a bank?
  • HMW help parents answer small questions late at night without sending them to a forum?
  • HMW let grandparents and partners add to a baby's memories without joining another app?
  • HMW make parents trust AI-written guidance? Proposed

Jobs to be done

  • When I am tired and just fed the baby, I want to record it in seconds so I can go back to rest.
  • When it is time for a check-up, I want the right vaccine schedule for my country so I do not miss one.
  • When I think about the future, I want guidance on money for my country so I feel prepared.
  • When something special happens, I want to save it and write to my child so they can read it later.
