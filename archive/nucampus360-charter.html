Purpose

Incoming students decide on a campus they have never walked. A brochure or a flat video does not answer the questions they actually have. NuCampus360 lets a student explore real campus locations in 360 degrees, see them on a map, and ask an AI guide the questions a map cannot answer. Confirmed
