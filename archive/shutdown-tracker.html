Shutdown and slowdown tracker

A planned shutdown touches eight production areas, dozens of owners and several functions. I built one workbook that holds every activity, shows what is late, and gives leadership a single front page.

Role
Built and maintained it as a project management intern
Built in
Excel, using formulas and conditional formatting only
Used by
Operations leadership
Skills shown
Program management, schedule and milestone tracking, risk and issue tracking, stakeholder coordination, KPI reporting, Excel

The problem

Shutdown work moves through approvals, permits, cleaning, maintenance, validation and a conditional release before a line can restart. Status sat in area-level lists, and a weekly slide pulled it together by hand.

What I built

The front page shows overall status and progress by area and by building. A timeline tab tracks the early milestones that gate everything else, such as approvals and work permits. A monthly calendar shows only the ramp down and ramp up for each area, because the weekly slide used that format and people already read it. Each area then has its own tab with the activity list and its own day by day calendar, and a rollup tab gathers every activity in one place.

How it works

Every activity carries one of four statuses: Not Started, In Progress, Completed or Blocked. Formulas compare end dates with today and flag anything late, so delay detection needs no one to remember to do it. A period selector switches the calendar between week, month, quarter and year. Gantt views follow the dates in the sheet through conditional formatting, which means changing a date moves the bar.

How I checked it

I audited the workbook formula by formula. The pass that mattered found nine leftover rows in the rollup that a visual check had missed, and three activities whose status no longer matched the calendar date. I also unified fonts, colors and print margins so every tab looked like it came from the same hand.

What it changed

The team got one place to look instead of area lists and a slide. Blocked and late items showed up while there was still time to act on them, and preparing the weekly update took far less effort than assembling it manually.
