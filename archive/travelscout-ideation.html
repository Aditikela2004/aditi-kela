Problem statement

Groups plan trips across chat threads, spreadsheets and links, and nobody trusts that prices are current. How might one shared room hold the conversation, the preferences, the options and the plan, with prices that show when and where they were checked? Confirmed

How might we

  • HMW get every traveler's needs on the table before options are proposed?
  • HMW let a group decide without a 200-message thread?
  • HMW show prices plainly?
  • HMW keep the plan near the conversation?
  • HMW handle money without awkwardness?

Jobs to be done

  • When I plan a trip with friends, I want everyone's needs in one place so I do not guess.
  • When we compare flights, I want to see how recent the price is so I can trust it.
  • When we disagree, I want a quick vote so we move on.
