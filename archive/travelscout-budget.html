  • Price and source lookups are extra and not included. Estimate

Cost controls

  • Cache price results for the checked date so repeated questions do not re-search.
  • Use the smaller model for routine chat; keep the larger model for plan building.
  • Cap turns per room per day.
  • Set a monthly spending limit with an alert.
