Gap and opportunity

  • Tour vendors sell 360 content but none of the vendor pages read showed an AI guide inside the tour.
  • Chat vendors sell AI answers but none of those pages showed 360 or map views.
  • NuCampus360 combines the two for one campus. For Seattle, no virtual tour was found. Sourced

Where competitors are stronger

  • Scale and trust: YouVisit claims 700+ partners; Ocelot claims 500+ institutions (vendor claims).
  • Integrations with CRM and student information systems.
  • Analytics portals and multi-language support.

Implications

  • Do not compete as a platform. Offer a lightweight pilot for one campus. Proposed
  • Lead with the combination: see the space, ask the question. Proposed
  • Offer admissions a view of the questions students ask. Proposed
  • Ask for quotes from YouVisit and Concept3D so pricing claims can be compared. Proposed
