Goals

  • Learn how first-time parents track feeds, sleep, diapers, growth and vaccines today.
  • Test whether country-specific guidance (units, vaccine schedule, finance topics) matters to them.
  • Find out how they keep memories and who they would share a time capsule with.
  • Decide whether the chatbot and the shared time capsule deserve to be built next.

Screener questions

  1. Do you have a child under 18 months, or are you expecting your first? (Continue if yes.)
  2. Is this your first child? (Prefer yes; include at most 2 second-time parents.)
  3. Which country do you live in now? Which country did you grow up in?
  4. Which apps, notes or paper do you use to track your baby today?
  5. Are you comfortable being recorded for note-taking? You can stop at any time. (Required)

Recruitment

  • Message friends of friends, student groups and community groups with a short note and the screener link. Proposed
  • Offer a small thank-you, such as a gift card. Amount to be set by the budget. Proposed
  • Do not recruit through the app's own users until the beta. Proposed

Consent and care

  • Explain the purpose, the recording and how notes are stored before starting.
  • Do not ask for the baby's medical records, photos or any identifying health detail.
  • Store notes under a participant code, not a name. Delete recordings after synthesis.
  • Offer to stop at any point. Treat parenting stress as sensitive and keep the tone warm.

TimeSectionPrompts

0:03Current habits (8 min)"Walk me through yesterday." "What do you write down, and where?" "What did you stop tracking and why?"
0:11Guidance and trust (8 min)"When you had a question about feeding, sleep or vaccines, where did you look?" "Was anything wrong for where you live?" "Have you thought about saving or planning money for your baby? Where did you find advice?"
0:19Memories (4 min)"How do you keep photos and moments?" "Who sees them?" "Would you write something to read in the future? To whom?"
0:23Concept test (8 min)Show BabySteps. Five tasks below. Ask them to think aloud.
0:31Wrap-up (4 min)"What would make you use this weekly?" "What would make you delete it?" "Anything I should have asked?"

Survey (12 questions)

  1. Your country of residence (list).
  2. Baby's age band.
  3. First child? (yes/no)
  4. How do you track your baby today? (multi-select)
  5. How often do you track? (daily, a few times a week, rarely)
  6. Which topics did you search for guidance on? (feeding, sleep, vaccines, growth, money, other)
  7. How much do you trust online baby advice? (1 to 5)
  8. Has advice ever not applied where you live? (yes/no/unsure; if yes, describe)
  9. How important is a vaccine schedule for your country? (1 to 5)
  10. Would you use a chatbot for baby questions? (yes/maybe/no; why)
  11. Would you share a time capsule with family? (yes/maybe/no; with whom)
  12. Any other comment (optional).

Synthesis method

  1. Write each quote or observation on its own note, with the participant code.
  2. Group notes into themes on an affinity map. Name each theme as a sentence.
  3. Count how many participants raised each theme. A theme needs at least 3 participants to count as a pattern.
  4. Turn each pattern into an insight, then into a "How might we" question.
  5. Compare insights with the scope document and propose changes through change control.
