Purpose

Group trips are planned across chat threads, spreadsheets and links, and prices go stale. TravelScout gives each trip one room. Everyone joins with a trip code, shares budget, interests, pace and dietary needs, and plans together in one chat with an AI travel guide that proposes flights and stays with price estimates, a checked date and a source link. Confirmed
