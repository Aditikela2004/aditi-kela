What was done

  • Research was run at 8 campus locations in a single weekend. Confirmed
  • The research came before the design. The team designed from what people said on site, not from assumptions. Confirmed
  • The product choices that followed: explore locations interactively, and add a chatbot for questions a map cannot answer. Confirmed

Synthesis steps

  1. Put each observation on its own note with its location.
  2. Group notes by theme: orientation, food, study, social, support, logistics.
  3. Count how many locations raised each theme.
  4. Rank themes by how many locations raised them and how serious the problem was.
  5. Convert the top themes into chatbot test questions and location details.

Follow-up validation plan (proposed)

ItemPlan
Participants8 incoming or recently admitted students, a mix of international and domestic
Method20-minute session: 5 minutes on expectations, 10 minutes on the prototype, 5 minutes on questions and trust
TasksFind the dining area, find a quiet study space, ask a question about the campus, say what is missing
MeasuresTime to complete each task; number of questions answered correctly; ease rating 1 to 5; would they share the tour (yes/no)
ConsentExplain purpose, recording, and that nothing identifying is stored
DecisionContinue to pilot if at least 6 of 8 complete all tasks and say the tour helped them

Interview guide for the follow-up (20 minutes)

TimePrompts
0:00"Tell me how you decided on this campus." "What did you wish you had seen before arriving?"
0:05Give the prototype. "Find the dining area." "Find a place to study quietly." Ask them to think aloud.
0:15"Ask the guide something you wondered about." "Did the answer help? Did you trust it?"
