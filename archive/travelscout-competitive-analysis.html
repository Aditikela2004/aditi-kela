Where competitors are stronger

  • Wanderlog: maps, real-time editing and email import, with mobile apps.
  • TripIt: reservation import and flight alerts.
  • Mindtrip: booking inside the app and price-drop alerts.
  • Splitwise: a mature expense engine.

Where TravelScout can stand apart

  • An AI guide in the group chat that asks first and shows its source, date and an estimate label.
  • Preferences that include dietary and accessibility needs.
  • Polls next to the conversation.
  • The gap on dated, sourced prices is a gap in what I read, not proof that no competitor does it.

Implications

  • Lead with trust: every price carries a date and a source. Proposed
  • Do not try to outdo Wanderlog on maps. Proposed
  • Add email import and a share link after group tests. Proposed
  • Keep expense splitting simple, or link out to Splitwise. Proposed
