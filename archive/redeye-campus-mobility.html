RedEye: campus mobility strategy

Our team analyzed why a campus transportation service fails students at peak times, and proposed an Agile roadmap to fix it.

Type
Team presentation
Course
Northeastern University graduate course
Frameworks
5-Why, jobs to be done, service blueprint, demand management, Kano model, Agile risk management, 12-month roadmap with KPIs
My role
Research, presentation architect and lead editor

What I did

  • Ran background research on RedEye's operational failures and turned raw observations into the problem framing that opened the deck.
  • Identified the intelligence gap, not a resource shortage, as the root cause. That became the central argument the framework analyses built on.
  • Built the presentation from the ground up: slide structure, formatting consistency, and integration of each teammate's section into one deliverable.
  • Wrote the appendix that maps each course framework to a specific RedEye failure mode and solution pillar.

The team's approach

  • A 5-Why analysis traced student wait times through fleet undersizing and static capacity allocation to ecosystem isolation.
  • A multi-framework lens used jobs to be done, a service blueprint, demand management and the Kano model on the same problem.
  • A twelve-month, four-phase roadmap: Stabilise, Integrate, Optimise and Scale, with a KPI dashboard and three decisions for executives, each tied to a Sprint 1 KPI.

What I learned

Owning both the research and the editing concentrated the effort. Next time I would ask for early peer input before frameworks are final, and delegate formatting work sooner so teammates own more of the finished piece.
