CAPA triage assistant

A concept tool that gives a quality reviewer a consistent first pass on a deviation report: a proposed severity with the reasons shown, a list of missing intake fields, and a draft CAPA outline. A named person approves every triage.

Why I built it

I worked around regulated manufacturing documentation at Grifols, where a missing field or an unclear record slows everything after it. This concept explores one narrow piece of that: what a reviewer could see on their first pass. The sample cases are synthetic and contain no company data.

The problem

Deviation intake is often thin, and severity calls can vary by reviewer. That pushes basic detail-chasing onto the reviewer and delays the investigation.

What I built

  • Three-step flow: intake, triage result, approval with an audit log kept in the browser.
  • Rules that propose a severity and category and show every rule that fired.
  • A documentation gap check mapped to ALCOA+ attributes.
  • A draft CAPA outline: containment, investigation, root cause hypotheses, corrective and preventive actions, effectiveness check.
  • An optional AI second opinion, kept separate from the rules-based result.

Decisions and tradeoffs

  • Rules first, AI second. A reviewer can trace why a severity was proposed, which matters when work is audited. The AI adds a comment and never changes the result.
  • Human approval is mandatory. The tool proposes and a named person decides.
  • Blank fields are allowed on purpose so the gap check can be demonstrated.
  • Illustrative timelines, labeled as placeholders, because real targets belong to each organization's own procedures.

What I tested

  • Sixteen unit tests, a production build and a smoke test of the page and API route.

What I have not tested yet

  • No real users. Keyword rules have not been checked against real historical deviations.
  • The audit log lives in the browser for demonstration. A real one needs server-side, tamper-evident storage and sign-in.

What I would do next

  • Walk three to five quality reviewers through the sample cases and record where they disagree with the proposed severity.
  • Replace keyword rules with criteria from a real procedure.
  • Move the audit log to authenticated, server-side storage.
