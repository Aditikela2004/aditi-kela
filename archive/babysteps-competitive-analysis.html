Where competitors are stronger

  • Sleep prediction and sleep plans at Huckleberry, with several years of product polish.
  • Caregiver sync across devices at four of the five apps.
  • Reminders for vaccines and appointments at Sprout.
  • Community and comparison content at Glow.
  • A free tier that already covers everyday logging, so a new app has to offer something different.

Where BabySteps can stand apart

  • Guidance that follows the parent's country, including the vaccine schedule and finance topics. None of the five showed this in the pages read.
  • One place for tracking and memories, ending in a letter the child can read later.
  • A product designed and reviewed against a written spec, so features can be explained and defended.

Implications

  • Lead the story with country-awareness, since that is the clearest gap. Proposed
  • Add caregiver sharing through the shared time capsule, then consider full sync. Proposed
  • Source and date every vaccine schedule in the app, since a wrong schedule would hurt trust. Proposed
  • Do not compete on sleep prediction. Proposed
  • Price near the market: paid tiers found run from about $5 to $10 a month. Sourced
