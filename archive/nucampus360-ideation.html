Problem statement

Incoming students must choose a campus they have never walked. Brochures and flat videos do not answer the questions they actually have. How might a virtual tour let them explore real places and get answers to their own questions? Confirmed

How might we

  • HMW let students see the places they will actually use, not the postcard views?
  • HMW answer small questions about the campus at any hour?
  • HMW show where a place is as well as what it looks like?
  • HMW help staff learn what students ask most?
  • HMW keep answers correct when the campus changes?
