Scope in one paragraph

BabySteps is a web app that helps first-time parents track their baby's days, watch growth and health, read guidance for the baby's age, and keep memories. The parent's country decides the measurement units, the vaccine schedule and the regional guidance, including finance and savings topics. Confirmed

In scope

  • Country-aware profile, units, vaccine schedule and guidance for the US and India Confirmed
  • Daily log, growth and health charts, age-aware guides, finance and savings topics Confirmed
  • Memory vault with photos, videos and a locked time-capsule letter Confirmed
  • Chatbot and shared time capsule, the next release Confirmed
  • Parent research, closed beta and an accuracy review of health content Proposed

Out of scope

  • Diagnosis, treatment advice or any replacement for a pediatrician Proposed
  • Native iOS or Android apps. The product is a web app. Confirmed
  • Telehealth, appointments or messaging with clinicians Proposed
  • Selling products, a marketplace or in-app payments for goods Proposed
  • Pregnancy tracking, since the product starts at birth Proposed
  • A public community forum Proposed

Acceptance criteria

AreaThe feature is accepted when
Country settingsChoosing a country changes the units, the vaccine schedule and the guidance on the next screen without a reload
Daily logA parent can record a diaper, feed or sleep entry in two taps from home, and it appears in the day's summary
GrowthA weight or height entry plots on the chart for the chosen standard (WHO for India)
GuidesGuides shown match the baby's age and the chosen country
Time capsuleA letter cannot be opened before its unlock date
ChatbotAnswers stay on non-diagnostic topics and point to a clinician for medical questions

Constraints and assumptions

  • One product lead; the AI writes the code under written specs. Confirmed
  • Budget stays near zero cash cost until beta. See the budget document. Estimate
  • Vaccine schedules come from published national schedules and must be reviewed for currency before each release. Proposed

Change control

  1. Write the change as one sentence plus the reason.
  2. Score it with MoSCoW and note the effort in days.
  3. If it is a Must or removes risk, add it and move something of equal size out.
  4. Otherwise, add it to the backlog and review at the next gate.
  5. Record the decision and date in this document.
