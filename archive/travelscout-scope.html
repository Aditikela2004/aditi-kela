Scope in one paragraph

One room per trip. Travelers join with a code, set their preferences and plan together in one chat with an AI travel guide. Tabs hold the plan, polls, itinerary and expenses so decisions stay near the conversation. Confirmed

Out of scope

  • Booking and payment inside the app Proposed
  • Visa, insurance or medical advice Proposed
  • Native mobile apps Proposed
  • Loyalty points and rewards tracking Proposed
  • Guaranteeing any price Confirmed

Acceptance criteria

AreaAccepted when
JoinA new traveler joins with the code and sets preferences in under 3 minutes
AI guideThe guide asks at least the destination, dates and budget before proposing options, and every option has a source link
PricesEvery price shows a date and is labeled as an estimate
PollsA poll created by one traveler is visible to all and shows results
ItineraryChosen items appear on the correct day

Constraints

  • Solo builder, coursework origin. Confirmed
  • Price sources depend on what the data tools can reach. To confirm
  • Costs must stay small while users are few. Estimate

Change control

  1. Write the change and the reason.
  2. Score with MoSCoW.
  3. Add only Musts, or swap with an equal item.
  4. Record the decision.
