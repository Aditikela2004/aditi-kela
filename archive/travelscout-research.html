Goals

  • Understand how groups of friends or family plan a trip today.
  • Find where planning breaks down: choosing dates, budget, places, food, who pays.
  • Test whether sourced, dated price estimates build trust.
  • Learn whether an AI guide in the group chat is welcome.

Screener

  1. Have you planned or joined a trip with 3 or more people in the past 12 months?
  2. Who organized it, and how many people went?
  3. Which tools did you use?
  4. Do you or anyone in your group have dietary or accessibility needs that affect planning?
  5. Are you comfortable being recorded for notes?

Interview guide (30 minutes)

TimePrompts
0:00Opening, consent, "Tell me about your last group trip."
0:05"How did the idea turn into a plan?" "Where did people disagree?" "What did you wish you had earlier?"
0:13"How did you pick flights and stays?" "How did you know prices were current?" "Did price change after you decided?"
0:20"How did you handle food and accessibility needs?" "How did you split costs?"
0:25"Would you let an AI suggest options in your group chat? What would make you trust it?"
0:28Wrap-up and thanks.

Group test script (45 minutes)

  1. Organizer creates a trip with destination, dates, travelers and budget.
  2. Others join with the code and fill their preferences.
  3. The group asks the guide for flights and stays; ask them to decide whether to trust each price.
  4. The group creates one poll and picks the winner.
  5. They add two items to the itinerary and check the weather.
  6. Debrief: "What was confusing?" "What would you use again?"

Survey (10 questions)

  1. How many group trips in the last year?
  2. Group size.
  3. Tools used for planning (multi-select).
  4. Biggest planning frustration (open).
  5. How do you decide (poll, one person decides, discussion)?
  6. How do you check price accuracy?
  7. Would you trust an AI-suggested price with a date and source? (1 to 5)
  8. Do you or your group have dietary or accessibility needs?
  9. How do you split expenses?
  10. Would you use a shared planning room? (yes/maybe/no; why)

Consent and care

  • Explain recording and storage; use participant codes.
  • Do not ask for passport, payment or booking details.
  • Delete recordings after synthesis.
