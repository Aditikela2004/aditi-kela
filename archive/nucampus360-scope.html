Scope in one paragraph

NuCampus360 is a virtual tour of campus locations in 360 degrees. Each location shows a campus map, details and an Ask AI button. The chatbot answers the questions a map cannot. Confirmed

Out of scope

  • Applications, enrollment or payment Proposed
  • Live human chat Proposed
  • VR headset experience Proposed
  • Indoor navigation and wayfinding Proposed
  • Other campuses until Seattle is validated Proposed

Acceptance criteria

  • Each location opens its 360 view, map position, details and Ask AI button in under 3 seconds on a normal connection. Proposed
  • Ten test questions drawn from the research all get a correct answer or a safe handoff. Proposed
  • A first-time visitor finds a dining area view without help. Proposed

Assumptions and constraints

  • One weekend for the prototype; the pilot is a separate decision. Confirmed
  • 360 content must be captured on site, with permission. Proposed
  • Chatbot answers depend on content provided by campus staff. Proposed

Change control

  1. Write the change and the reason.
  2. Check it against the research question it serves.
  3. Score it with MoSCoW.
  4. Add it only if it is a Must or replaces something of the same size.
  5. Record the decision.
