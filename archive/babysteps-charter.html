Purpose

New parents collect advice from many places, and much of it ignores where they live. Food guidance, vaccine schedules, growth standards and financial advice change by country, yet most baby apps give one answer to everyone. BabySteps puts tracking and memory-keeping in one app and uses the parent's country to set units, the vaccine schedule and regional guidance. Confirmed

Risks

RiskLikelihoodImpactMitigation
Health guidance or vaccine schedules are wrong or out of dateMediumHighSource each schedule from the national body (for India, the IAP; for the US, the CDC), show the source in the app and review before each release
AI guidance reads as medical adviceMediumHighLabel guidance as information, add a "talk to your pediatrician" line, limit the chatbot to non-diagnostic topics
Baby and family data is sensitiveMediumHighCollect the minimum, explain what is stored, support deletion, review hosting settings before beta
Sign-in blocks people from seeing the appHighMediumOffer a demo account and a walkthrough video
AI usage cost grows faster than usersLowMediumUse the smaller model for routine guidance and set a monthly cost cap
Solo builder timeHighMediumKeep each phase to three weeks or less and cut scope before moving a date

Assumptions and constraints
