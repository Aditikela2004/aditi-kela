About

I am based in Seattle, WA, and finishing an M.S. in Information Systems at Northeastern University. Before that I spent three years in risk and compliance at RBL Bank in Mumbai. From June to September 2026 I worked on the Operational Excellence team at Grifols Biologicals in Los Angeles.

My work follows one pattern. I find a process that people run by hand, write down what it needs to do, and build the tracker, workflow or report that does it. In a bank that meant regulatory checks and approvals. In a manufacturing plant it meant project status, dependencies and shutdown coordination.

I also build application projects to practice product work: a group travel chatbot, an AI parenting app, a service request prototype and a clinic records app. The Work page shows each one with its current status.

I am looking for technical program and project delivery roles, and associate product roles that use the same planning and requirements skills.

Languages: English, Hindi and Marathi.
