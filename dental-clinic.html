Dental Clinic app

A Swift iOS app for patient records and billing. It makes it easier to store records, track returning patients and manage invoices.

Type
iOS app built in Swift with Xcode
Context
College-sponsored project for a dental clinic
Status
Live, in day-to-day use by clinic staff for about 150 patients
Privacy
This page shows no patient data

What it does

  • Stores patient records in one place that the doctor and clinic staff can reach easily.
  • Tracks returning patients so staff can see who has visited before.
  • Manages invoices as part of the same app, so billing and records are not kept separately.

Limitations

  • I do not publish a time-saving figure for this app because I have no measurement behind one.
  • The patient count is approximate.
